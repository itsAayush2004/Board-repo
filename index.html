Two game apps

Engines I built, then games on top.

HexaBed

A procedural hex-grid terrain engine in Unity, plus the editor tooling that lets a designer build a world without opening a script.

  • 160+ hex tiles generated into one playable world with varied heights
  • Road generator picks straight, turn, ramp or pit pieces from neighbouring tile heights
  • HexTileEditor: a custom inspector with 60° snapping and GLB export (25+ world meshes)
