Website 02 · ARTHIS.land

The Wall: a city of rooms you build and share.

A handcrafted vertical city in the browser. Unity, shipped to WebGL, where every brick on the wall is a room someone built, and 2–4 people can explore a space together in real time.

1Build your bed
2Create your avatar
3Edit the blocks

Rooms on the wall

My Room · Aashna Room · Bindo + Pinky Room · Dada Room · Mummy Papa Room · Kitchen · Mandir

  • Host a room and invite friends with a 4-digit code
  • Each room is a folder: a GLB build, a thumbnail and a room.json; the city lays itself out from them

Game engines & apps

The tools under the games.

HexaBed

A procedural hex-grid terrain engine in Unity, plus editor tooling so a designer can build a world without opening a script.

  • 160+ hex tiles generated into one playable world with varied heights
  • Road generator picks straight, turn, ramp or pit pieces from neighbouring tile heights
  • HexTileEditor inspector with 60° snapping and GLB export (25+ world meshes)
UnityC#14 scripts3,705 lines

HexGrid Architect private repo

A 3D hex-grid builder in the browser: select ground tiles, press Build, then stack houses, towers and roofs layer by layer. React Three Fiber with custom GLB hex pieces.
